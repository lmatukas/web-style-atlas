# Visual acceptance rubric — v1

Frozen after the three-direction P04 pilot; see `docs/evidence/pilot.md`.
This is an editorial quality rule, not an objective beauty score.

Evaluate both compositions at desktop/mobile and inspect all four actual PNGs.
Record the environment, filenames, observable evidence and nearest available
comparison. Planned neighbors must be identified as future work. An unavailable
browser or inability to see images is `not_run`, never a visual PASS.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recognition | No distinct direction | One/two visible traits | At least three stated signature traits visibly present |
| Neighbor distinction | Recolored duplicate | Cosmetic changes | Three dimension differences, including composition/navigation/rhythm |
| A/B distinction | Same layout | Reordered or resized parts | Different composition and content task, same recognizable signature |
| Hierarchy | Collisions or no reading path | Inconsistent hierarchy | Clear title/body/caption roles and deliberate reading order |
| Mobile | Cropping/unusable controls | Desktop merely shrunk | Intentional reflow, readable text and operable controls |
| Adaptability | Topic/asset is the entire design | Transfer guidance incomplete | Another brief can preserve the signature with concrete instructions |

Accept at least 10/12, no zero, with recognition and distinction both 2.
A good score cannot excuse broken controls, invalid files, unresolved rights,
failed contrast/resource checks or stale previews. Fix those independently.

For every new style, record 3–5 invariants and exactly three direction-specific
anti-patterns. Compare the closest available neighbor in the same viewport.
Use one neutral brief across close directions when subject matter might conceal
a shared template. Do not require equal hero/features/testimonials/CTA sections.

Record template:

- Author/task/commit and actual checks.
- A desktop/mobile observations and B desktop/mobile observations.
- Nearest actual style; three dimension differences, at least one structural.
- Six scores with concrete reasons and remaining limitations.
- Rights/resource/keyboard/reduced-motion checks, distinct from visual judgment.

The pilot accepted Swiss Grid, Editorial Journal and Neo Brutalist at 12/12 each.
These are reviewed baseline examples, not an instruction to auto-award later
styles the same score. Reassess when a closer neighbor exists or content changes.
