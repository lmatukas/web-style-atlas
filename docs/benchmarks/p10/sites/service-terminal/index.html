03 SERVICES / OPEN TO INSPECT
01   copy-editingImprove clarity, flow and consistency.+

INPUT A complete draft and a sense of who needs to read it.

OUTPUT Edited copy with clearer sentences, consistent terms and queries where your judgment is needed.

SCOPE We agree the level of editing before work begins; new research or writing is separate.

02   proofreadingCatch errors in a near-final document.+

INPUT A final or nearly final file with layout already in place.

OUTPUT Corrections for spelling, punctuation, grammar and visible consistency issues.

SCOPE Proofreading is a final check, not a substitute for substantive editing or fact-checking.

03   document structureMake information easier to find and follow.+

INPUT A draft or a set of material that feels difficult to navigate.

OUTPUT A proposed order, headings and signposts, with notes on gaps or repetition.

SCOPE Structure recommendations are agreed before any full rewrite.
