STILLWATER / PORTFOLIO

A practice of
close attention.

Stillwater is a fictional landscape photographer’s portfolio, shaped around two ongoing studies: the softened edges of the forest and the changing surface of water.

The photographs shown here are rights-cleared examples bundled with Web Style Atlas. They are credited to their original creators and are not presented as photographs made by Stillwater.

SERIES 01 — FOREST

A softer
kind of distance.

In the forest, detail arrives gradually. A familiar outline dissolves, then returns in another place.

SERIES 02 — WATER

The last light
held still.

Water keeps a second version of the sky. At dusk, the boundary between them nearly disappears.

Prepare a useful project brief +
  • What is the project, exhibition, or publication?
  • Which landscapes, locations, or themes should it explore?
  • What format and image count do you have in mind?
  • What are the intended dates, audience, and usage?
  • What budget range and licensing needs should be discussed?

When a real contact channel is available, these details help make the first conversation specific.
