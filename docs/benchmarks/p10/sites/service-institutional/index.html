SERVICE DETAILS

Practical help, matched to the document

Copy-editing

For a draft that says the right things but needs a clearer route through them. Edits focus on wording, consistency and readability.

Proofreading

For writing that is already settled and needs a careful final check. The focus is on errors and small corrections, not a full rewrite.

Document structure

For information that feels difficult to navigate. We can review the order, headings and signposting before sentence-level editing.

A CLEAR, SMALL-SCALE PROCESS

Three steps from draft to hand-off

Each project starts with a shared understanding of the document and what a useful result looks like.

  1. 01

    Share a sample

    Choose a short excerpt and note who reads it and what they need to do. A sample gives both sides a practical starting point.

  2. 02

    Agree a scope

    Set the document, type of edit, timing and hand-off format before any work begins. The scope should be clear and manageable.

  3. 03

    Review and hand off

    Review the edited draft and notes, resolve any open questions, then receive the files in the agreed format.

A USEFUL PLACE TO BEGIN

Start with one short sample.

Pick a page or a few paragraphs that represent the document. Add a sentence about its readers and the change you want it to make. That is enough to begin a conversation about fit and scope.
