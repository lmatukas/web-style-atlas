## Scope and limitations

The workflow intentionally checks the exact 0.1.0 twelve-style inventory. A
future release must update its version/count expectations before publication.
Native Codex/Claude/Bob runtime claims stay at the P09/P10 evidence level;
automated accessibility checks do not prove complete WCAG conformance.
