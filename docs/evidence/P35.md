# P35 — S58 primary and secondary actions (P27-A07, #144)

Author: agent/01dpob2fr2xg

Checked: 2026-10-24

## Cause and change

In `velvet-members-hall` A, the reservation submit “Paruošti rezervaciją” and
the membership navigation “Pradėti pažintį” both used the bordered `.primary`
style. Where the evening reservation and the membership section share a
screen, two equal primary actions were visible at once.

The reservation submit stays the one primary action. “Pradėti pažintį” is now
a `.secondary-link`: an underlined text link in the accent colour, 44 px tall
for touch, with the same destination (`alternate.html#application`). Both
services remain reachable; nothing else changed.

around the form still has at most one primary action.
