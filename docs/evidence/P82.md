# P82 — De-template copy, batch 1

Author: agent/015fh6w2uenf

Ten directions were rewritten with the fix prompt in section 6 of
`docs/audits/ai-template-audit.md`. Layout, palette, type, motion, images,
A/B structure and every working function are unchanged.

What changed in each direction:

- Headlines are plain sentences built on a fact from the page (a size, a
  count, a material, a year, a room). Line breaks stay where the design
  puts them, and first-screen headlines were kept close to the old length
  so they fit the same space on desktop and phone.
- Mood words ("quiet", "calm", "slow", "unhurried", "no hurry", "seamless")
  were replaced with the direction's own subject: knots and cabins, rituals
  and nights, kHz and grams, rooms and seat counts, modules and joints.
- Honesty is one footer line saying the brand is invented, plus one short
  note beside each form or button that could look real. The extra
  "demonstration / illustrative / invented" mentions were removed, and the
  confirmation texts still say plainly that nothing was booked, sent or paid.
- Uppercase labels that repeated the heading or numbered nothing were
  replaced with labels that carry information or removed.
- Arrows were removed from in-page links and buttons; links that open the
  other page keep theirs. Em dashes in ranges became en dashes, and joiners
  in dynamic text became colons.
- Velvet Members Hall used a real city for one of its houses; it is now the
  invented "Old Town house" (internal value `oldtown`).
- Each creation prompt now quotes the new headline where it quoted the old
  one and states the voice and honesty rule for that direction.
- The ten IDs were removed from `COPY_BACKLOG` (32 remain, still sorted).
