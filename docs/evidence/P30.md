# P30 — S55 B facade and booking action (P27-A02, #139)

Author: agent/01dpob2fr2xg

Checked: 2026-10-24

## Cause and change

1. **Facade height.** The B `.band` had `min-height:54svh` (56svh on phones),
   so the image was 473/844 px on phones and 540/1000 px on larger screens.
   It is now `76svh` at every width. The title, date chip and gradient stay at
   the bottom of the image.
2. **Two primary actions.** The fixed “Rezervuoti” tab in `booking.mjs`
   watched only the masthead. Once the masthead scrolled away, it reappeared
   even while a bordered “Rinktis šį kambarį” button was on screen. The same
   observer now watches the masthead and every primary booking button inside
   `main`, and the tab shows only while none of them is visible. Secondary
   (ghost) room buttons do not hide it, so a booking route is always visible.
   The drawer, its focus trap and its logic are unchanged.
