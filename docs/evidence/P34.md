# P34 — S54 keyboard and range QA (P27-A06, #143)

Author: agent/01dpob2fr2xg

Checked: 2026-10-24

## Diagnosis

This was a real page defect, not a flaky test. In `reference/main.mjs`,
`scrollReveal` ties the mechanism reveal to the scroll position inside the
story section. Focusing the scene or the `#reveal` range with the keyboard can
move the page, and the browser delivers that scroll event a frame later, after
the key press has already set the reveal. `scrollReveal` then wrote the
scroll-derived value over the reader's choice.

Reproduction on the unchanged page (390×844, normal motion, both real and
frozen clock): sweeping the scroll position in 150 px steps, focusing the
scene and pressing End, failed at 2 positions in `index.html` with
reveal = 0.016, the same value P27 recorded (0.0157). The range path failed
the same way (P27: 0.03 instead of 0.01). `alternate.html` did not reproduce
it at those positions. The unmodified full `qa.mjs` failed 1 of 3 local runs.

## Fix

A manual reveal choice (scene keys, range, toggle button, drag) now records
the scroll position at that moment. `scrollReveal` ignores scroll events while
the page is still within 24 px of that position, and hands control back to
scrolling as soon as the reader actually scrolls further. The choice is no
longer overwritten by its own focus scroll, and scroll-driven reveal still
works afterwards. No assertion was removed, relaxed or retried.

## New regression check

`qa-interaction.mjs` adds a sweep for A and B at 390×844 and 1440×1000: at
every 150 px scroll position it focuses the scene and presses End, then
focuses the range and presses Home + ArrowRight, and asserts 1 and 0.01. It
then confirms that a real later scroll drives the reveal again (> 0.4).

- With the old `main.mjs`: **FAIL** at `index/390: End after focus from y=300`.
- With the fix: **PASS** for all four page/width combinations.
