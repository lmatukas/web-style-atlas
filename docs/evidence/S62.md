## Ten luxury conditions

1. **Large first image:** A at 390 has a 793px scene in an 844px viewport;
   desktop 940/1000. B is 743/844 and 820/1000. The local photo covers the full
   scene; the plate owns the broad right-hand composition and the phone crop.
2. **OFL and labels:** two families, three files, 400 regular/true italic,
   11–13px tracked labels; source glyph/rights/budget checks pass.
3. **Signature works:** actual scroll and previous/next update A's course,
   time and photo; native moment controls update B by keyboard/mouse/touch.
4. **One main action:** one hero reservation action or one service confirmation
   per visible section. Navigation, pause and choices remain secondary.
5. **Real local service:** date/time/party alter availability; table capacity,
   required choices, errors, exact summary, edit and reset were exercised.
6. **Bounded motion:** arrival settles in 1.5s; pause and reduced motion work;
   no autoplay loop, scroll lock, orientation access or background audio.
7. **Static path:** no 3D/WebGL is claimed or required. Actual local photos and
   all semantic menu/service content are the static reduced/blocked-API path.
8. **Phone composition:** dedicated image crop and intentional column stacking;
   all four specified widths passed real browser inspection.
9. **Accessibility/resources:** eight clean axe pages, token contrasts, real
   keyboard/touch operation, local fonts/images and source budgets passed.
10. **Distinct identity:** the first candlelit plate and subsequent clock/room
    service are immediately distinguishable from both inspected neighbors.
