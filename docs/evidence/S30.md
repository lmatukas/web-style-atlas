## Rights and limits

All meshes, shaders, SVGs and copy are original MIT work. Arial/Helvetica are
system font requests; no fonts, imported models, photos or audio are bundled.
These are illustrative light studies, not photorealistic or physically measured
renderings. Storage is browser-local and not synchronized to another device.
The SVG fallback preserves scene choice and route functions but makes no live-3D
claim. No optional audio, server, account, booking or public deployment added.
