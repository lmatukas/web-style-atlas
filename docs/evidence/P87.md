## F8 — Glow that follows the cursor

The audit listed nine directions. Each was checked against its prompt and
code:

| Direction | Pointer behaviour | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light Environments | Steers the light direction in the scene | Kept: the pointer controls the subject |
| Woven Field | Bends the thread field | Kept: the pointer controls the subject |
| Scent Ritual | Moves the liquid in the bottle (slider on phones) | Kept: the pointer controls the subject |
| Velvet Members Hall | Moves the light on the velvet (slider on phones) | Kept: documented "light the visitor controls" |
| Patina Auction Room | Sets the light-direction slider on the object | Kept: same control as the slider |
| Escapement Nocturne | Drag reveals the movement | Kept: a drag control, not a glow |
| Luminous Cut Vitrine | Drag turns the stone | Kept: a drag control, not a glow |
| Acoustic Sculpture | Shifted the light on the plates | **Removed**: atmosphere only, nothing on phones |
| Midnight Vestibule | Moved a lantern glow after the intro | **Removed**: the lamps now come on once and stay lit |

Acoustic Sculpture's `check.mjs` now asserts that the light stays fixed
when the pointer moves. Both prompts and `style.md` files describe the new
behaviour.

## F7 — One italic word in a headline

Twenty directions had an `<em>` in an h1 or h2. The computed style showed
which ones render as a decorative italic. Italic stays where it is a
documented type signature:

- Kept: Heirloom Weave Ledger and Velvet Members Hall (a separate true
  italic face for the second phrase of headings is their documented
  signature), Editorial Journal (italic serif accents are in its prompt),
  Choreographic Stage (italic in the opening promise is documented), Woven
  Field (documented occasional italic), Y2K Chrome (an Arial accent on the
  release title, not a serif italic word).
- Made upright, accent colour kept: Ember Course Room (headings only; course
  names stay italic as documented), Quiet Luxury, Organic Craft, Discovery
  Cabinet, Patina Auction Room, Seasonal Terrain, Twilight Expedition,
  Cinematic Photo, Field Notes.

The change is one CSS rule per direction (`h1 em, h2 em { font-style:
normal; }`), so line breaks and accent colours stay as they were. Each of
the nine prompts now says that heading accent words stay upright.
