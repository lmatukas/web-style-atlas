Author: agent/417f6ffda8ee. Date: 2026-10-24. Base: `7563eba1806e644eae42a3cbf5c382605dec2572`.

## Result and scope

New light luxury direction with two working references, four imaginary scents,
a native WebGL glass bottle and moving liquid, a three-question finder and
an editable 1–3 sample tray. Full Lithuanian creation prompt, local fonts,
asset rights and generated catalogue entries included. No shared schema,
gallery source, workflow, other style or actual commerce integration changed.

A pairs a narrow invitation with a broad bottle stage, then numbered scent
records, a three-layer note ledger and a sample atelier. B uses an immersive
full-width bottle stage followed by a question/pyramid pair and explained result.
The tray is deliberately shared service behaviour, not the main A/B composition.
