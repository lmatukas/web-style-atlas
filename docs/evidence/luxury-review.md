## Ten luxury conditions

labels; 3 = signature mouse/keyboard/touch interaction; 4 = one primary CTA in
the visible viewport; 5 = local service, errors/confirmation/reset; 6 = finite
motion, pause and complete reduced-motion view; 7 = tested WebGL fallback;
8 = intentional mobile composition; 9 = accessibility/contrast/resources;
10 = recognisable and distinct. P = observed pass, F = observed failure,
H = unresolved verification hold, — = no WebGL (static HTML/SVG still checked).
