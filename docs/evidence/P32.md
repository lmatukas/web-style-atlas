# P32 — S59 A phone vessel (P27-A04, #141)

Author: agent/01dpob2fr2xg

Checked: 2026-10-24

## Cause and change

On phones (`max-width:650px`) the A object scene started 200 px into the hero,
below the eyebrow, title, intro and button. The 710 px image therefore began
at y=300 and only 544 px (64.5%) was visible in the first screen. Moving the
scene up alone would have put the intro and button on top of the vessel.

`reference/style.css` now starts the phone scene at 120 px, directly under
the two-line title, and gives the intro a 540 px top margin so the intro and
“Atverti katalogą” follow the vessel's plinth instead of covering it. The
title stays above the vessel's lip; the spotlight, lot data, light controls
and B variant are unchanged.

## Measurement

Visible intersection of the A `.object-scene` with the first viewport,
`scrollY=0` (P27 method). Normal and reduced motion gave the same values.

| Viewport | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 360×844 | 544 px, 64.5% | 624 px, 73.9% |
| 390×844 | 544 px, 64.5% | 624 px, 73.9% |
| 768×1000 | 886 px, 88.6% | 886 px, 88.6% |
| 1440×1000 | 886 px, 88.6% | 886 px, 88.6% |

B stays at 85.3% (phones) and 85.0–88.6% (larger).
