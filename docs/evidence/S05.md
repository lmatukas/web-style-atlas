# S05 — Organic Craft

Original fictional Kilnrue ceramics direction. Four invariants: warm paper and
sand surfaces, uneven object silhouettes, asymmetric image/text relationships,
and alternating material observations and individual object studies. A tells
a making story with a dominant pitcher, material strip and numbered process.
B is an uneven object collection with a large pitcher, offset smaller bowl and
wide vase row, each with attached practical notes and native dimensions details.
There is no checkout, invented stock or claim these illustrations are photographs.

Three original code-authored SVG ceramic studies (pitcher, bowl, vase) use
shading, turning lines and clipped speckle patterns. All are registered as
original MIT illustrations with a bundled authorship/rights note. No third-party
images/fonts, external resources or large bitmap originals are required.
Semantic interface tokens are separate from the artwork palette.

Actually compared same-width previews with Editorial Journal and Swiss Grid.
Against Editorial: freestanding uneven object stages replace literary masthead
and reading column; making/object anchors replace department/essay navigation;
short material/process/object notes replace sustained literary rhythm. Against
Swiss: soft backplates, irregular silhouettes and unequal object scale replace
numbered ruled service rows and typographic geometry. The common warm serif
palette does not carry the distinction alone. Field Notes and Botanical
Engraving are unavailable planned neighbors, retained as future comparisons.
