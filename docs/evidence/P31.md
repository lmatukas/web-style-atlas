# P31 — S57 phone opening artwork (P27-A03, #140)

Author: agent/01dpob2fr2xg

Checked: 2026-10-24

## Cause and change

On phones the large `ES` monogram (32svh) and the archive object (37svh) sat
in two separate boxes with blank space between them, so the artwork read as
two small pictures and covered only about 65% of the first screen.

`reference/style.css` adds one phone rule set (`max-width:700px`) for both
variants:

- the monogram becomes a large field across the full width (62svh, ring
  touching the edges, opacity .2);
- the archive object sits at the foot of that field, full bleed, so mark and
  object form one composition in an 86svh opening;
- in B the drawn case is scaled so the case itself, not its transparent
  margin, fills the space;
- captions move under the image as normal text, so the historical image and
  the invented house mark stay clearly separate.

Tablet and desktop rules are unchanged.

## Measurement

This measures drawn artwork, not boxes: from the top of the monogram ring to
the bottom of the photograph (A) or of the drawn case inside `folio.svg` (B),
clipped to the first viewport. Reduced and normal motion gave the same values.

| Viewport | A before | A after | B before | B after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 360×844 | ~65% (32% + 37% boxes, gap between) | 74.7% | ~65% | 76.3% |
| 390×844 | ~65% | 74.7% | ~65% | 76.3% |
| 768×1000 | unchanged | unchanged | unchanged | unchanged |
| 1440×1000 | unchanged | unchanged | unchanged | unchanged |

No horizontal overflow at any of the four widths.
