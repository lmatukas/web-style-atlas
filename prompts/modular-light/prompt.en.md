The new main object is a lamp made of opaque folded paper and matte aluminium from five flat modules. The lamp's working construction and clear joints between the planes organise the form.

Palette: graphite #262B2B, butter yellow #F0D98B, warm white #F7F2E8 and sage #8EAA98. The first screen should recall a spread from a contemporary product catalogue: a vertical collection heading on the left, a large object in the centre and a clear "Build yours" on the right. Then present the module principle, three use scenarios for different spaces, care information and an interactive assembly. Write all text originally in English; mark technical properties clearly as concept settings.

Motion script:
• When the site opens, the five modules come together from a compact exploded view into the lamp within 900 ms. The final position stays stable.
• Switching between "For reading", "For evenings" and "For work" changes the cone of light, the warm room colour and the explanation. Do not use flicker or a bright flash.
• Pressing "How it fits together" separates the modules along one axis with numbered markers. A second press puts them back together; the information is also available as text.
• When a module is added in the configurator, the object briefly rearranges and the counter and size summary update. The action must be easy to undo.
