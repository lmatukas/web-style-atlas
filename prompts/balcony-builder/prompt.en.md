## Original creative brief

Create your own visual expression: original copy, objects, composition and motion sequence. Use only your own or properly licensed assets. Do not carry over other brands, code, recognisable scenes or combinations of them. Say once, in the footer, that the brand is invented, and once beside any form or download that nothing is sent. Headings name sun hours, modules and plants rather than slogans; use real facts only once the user provides reliable content.

Build a finished, working English website for the invented working brand "SPROUVEL", which offers growing kits for city balconies. Work on your own according to the user's project environment instructions and the website tools suited to it. Deliver a working private preview. Do not publish publicly without a separate instruction.

Build your own system: a rich plum-violet background, bright lime green, warm white and a coral accent; large rounded type and original playful botanical illustrations. On the first screen, put a clear promise on the left, "A garden fits on your balcony", and on the right a modular planter box seen from above. Then lay out a choice between three balcony situations, the kit contents, how it grows and common questions. Do not use an agency portfolio structure.

Build four purposeful motions. When the page opens, the parts of the box come together into one object within 800 ms and stop. When the balcony light is chosen, the box swaps to suitable plants within 500 ms and the explanation updates. Scrolling through the growing section, one illustrated plant branch gradually unfolds; once the section has been passed it no longer animates. When the kit is added to the choice, the contents list briefly expands and shows the result clearly. Avoid constant decorative distraction.

The main action is a three-step kit picker based on light, area and experience. Show a specific result, explain the choice and let the visitor save it on this device. No fake payments or supposedly sent requests. Mark clearly that this is a concept demonstration.

On a phone the box must fit on the screen and the choices must work by touch. Support the keyboard, visible focus, enough contrast and prefers-reduced-motion. If 3D is not supported, use an original static illustration with the same choice functions. The content must stay readable before the image has loaded. Use only your own or properly licensed assets and original English copy, with no lorem ipsum, invented clients or testimonials.

The work is done when the picker and saving work, there are no dead buttons or horizontal scrolling, the view has been checked on a phone and a computer, and a similarity review confirms your own object, structure and motion logic. At the end, give the preview link and briefly state the checks you ran. All HTML content and navigation are reachable without 3D. Assets and fonts must be your own, generated or clearly licensed for use; in the final answer briefly state their origin. Without real sending, do not show "sent".

A static screenshot alone does not prove that an animation or function works. If a required technology is unavailable, state the limit exactly; do not present a decorative fallback as checked 3D or sound.

Functional acceptance: light, area and experience produce a reasoned demonstration kit; the choices can be saved locally and changed.

Deliver a working preview, a summary of the checks actually run, the origin of assets and the remaining limits. Publish publicly only when the user asks. Finish the implementation; a plan alone is not a result.
