# Still Sanctuary — website creation prompt

Build a working website in the user's project. This is a self-contained creation prompt; do not change the atlas or its task registry.

## Adapt to the project

- Brand and business: [FILL IN OR KEEP THE DEMONSTRATION].
- Audience and main action: [FILL IN].
- Approved places, rituals, villas, dates and photographs: [FILL IN].
- Technology and project rules: [APPLY THE EXISTING ONES].
- Composition: [A OR B; default A].

If the fields stay empty, create an invented project of small retreats called "Auvrėja". Do not use real reviews, healing promises or invented awards. Keep the five signature features: a calm landscape that owns the first screen; warm sand with olive and clay light; a light serif with a humanist sans; a control for three time-of-day rituals that changes the photograph and the light of the page; a real local summary of the stay enquiry.

A: directions by region. After a large forest photograph and a short invitation, show three numbered landscape rows, each with a photograph, a short description and a link to choose that region. Then the daily rituals, three experiences and the enquiry. B: one place. An asymmetric first screen with a sand heading and a lake photograph, short facts, the daily rituals, descriptions of two villas with size and capacity, and the enquiry. B is not a reshuffled version of the A cards.

## Original creative brief

Write original copy and composition. Name places, times and rituals instead of announcing calm or slowness; one footer line says the brand is invented, and the form has one short note that nothing is booked. Do not copy real resort names, logos or websites. Use three to five legally shareable CC0 or public-domain photographs of nature and architecture. Photograph rights, authors and sources belong in the rights file; the example page does not send visitors to source websites. Photographs up to 1.5 MB, WebP, without metadata. Keep illustrative photographs clearly apart from any offer for a real place.

Palette: sand #f3eee7, deep brown text #322f25, olive #49543a, daylight #f0e5d4, evening clay #e9d4c4. A calm local OFL serif 400 for headings and a humanist sans 400 for text, up to 200 KB, WOFF2 with Lithuanian letters and full OFL.txt files. Small uppercase labels 11–14 px with 2 px spacing. Headings mostly 32–50 px, one campaign heading up to 76 px. Plenty of air, thin lines, no row of generic rounded cards.

On the first screen, one landscape fills at least 70% of the viewport height, on a phone too. A plain heading that names the places: "Retreats by a forest, a lake and a garden." One main stay action. On a phone, B has its own full-frame composition, not a shrunken two-column view.

Signature: three buttons, Morning, Day, Evening. Morning 07:00–10:00 shows the forest photograph, a walk and breathing; Day 12:00–16:00 the pavilion, a light sand background and quiet reading; Evening 18:00–21:00 the lake, a clay tone and tea. Each choice changes the real photograph, its alt text, the time, the heading, the description and the light of the page. The buttons work with a mouse, touch and keyboard and show a clear selected state.

Stay enquiry: the regions Forest valley, Lakeshore and Garden pavilions; arrival and departure dates; at least one of the rituals Forest walk, Breathing practice, Evening tea. Check missing fields, an arrival in the past, equal or reversed dates, more than 21 nights and an unconfirmed demonstration notice. Each error is named clearly and moves focus to its field. The confirmation names the exact region, dates, nights and rituals, and says this is a local demonstration where nothing was sent or booked. Changing a choice removes the old confirmation. Clear resets the form. No real sending, payment or booking.

Motion: a barely noticeable zoom and brightening of the photograph over 1.6 s, then complete stillness. A visible pause that resumes only the rest of the episode. Reduced motion shows the final image right away, with no RAF loop. Stop work when the window or the scene is hidden; on pagehide, free the observers. No WebGL or audio is needed.

## Final check

Check both layouts at 360, 390, 768 and 1440 px. Try all three rituals and the real changes in photograph and background. Check the same features with a keyboard and touch. Check every form error, the correct number of nights, the confirmation, its removal when a field changes, and clearing. Test pause, the reduced-motion setting, leaving the viewport and resource cleanup.

Actually review the four final A/B desktop and phone previews. Check contrast in all three lights, focus, Lithuanian letters, local font loading, the console and the absence of external requests. Judge the differences from a documentary travel notebook and a dark photo magazine: structure, typography, colour and the service flow. Deliver a working preview, a summary of the checks you actually ran and the remaining limits. Publish only when the user asks; a plan alone is not a result.
