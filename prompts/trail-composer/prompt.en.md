## Original creative brief

Create your own visual expression: original copy, objects, composition and motion sequence. Use only your own or properly licensed assets. Do not carry over other brands, code, recognisable scenes or combinations of them. Say once, in the footer, that the brand is invented, and once beside any form or download that nothing is sent. Headings name lengths, paces and moods as options, not "Fewer things. More attention." slogans; use real facts only once the user provides reliable content.

Build a working English website for the invented working travel project "KELIATA", made for slow one-day outings close to home. This is a demonstration concept, so do not create real offers or booking promises. Use the website-building flow of the user's project environment, decide on your own and finish a private preview. Do not publish publicly without a separate instruction.

Base the visual system on map paper: a very light yellowish tone, lake blue, moss green and small orange path markings. On the first screen, show a vertical stylised trail diagram, a clear idea, "Plan a day out of three, five or seven hours", and a visible route choice. Then lay out an outing mood picker, three original demonstration routes, a timeline of the chosen plan, a preparation list and common questions. Use original diagrams or properly licensed photographs with accurate descriptions of the places.

Plan four animations. On entering the page, the trail line draws itself to the first stop within 900 ms and stops. When a mood is chosen, the matching route points stand out within 300 ms while the text explanation updates. When a route is opened, its stops unfold into a vertical timeline within 450 ms, keeping a clear way back. When a preparation item is ticked, a short mark animation confirms the state and ends at once. Do not use forced horizontal scrolling or important content that depends on the mouse.

The main action is to choose the outing length, the pace and the mood, then get a suitable example day plan. Let the visitor change the choices, tick off the preparation and download the plan as a text file. Mark approximate times; do not present the demo plan as a checked offer from a real organiser. Do not imitate a booking or a sent email.

On a phone, show one clear vertical flow with comfortable touch targets. Ensure keyboard control, visible focus, contrast and prefers-reduced-motion. If the interactive diagram is not supported, all stops must remain in a plain readable list. For this site, choose light SVG or CSS solutions if they achieve the idea without 3D. All copy is in English and original, with no lorem ipsum, invented testimonials or awards.

The work is done when choosing, opening the plan, the preparation list and the download work on a phone and a computer. Check empty states, reduced motion and the absence of horizontal overflow. Deliver a working preview and a short result of the checks. All HTML content and navigation are reachable without 3D. Assets and fonts must be your own, generated or clearly licensed for use; in the final answer briefly state their origin. Without real sending, do not show "sent".
