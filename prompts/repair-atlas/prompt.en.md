## Original creative brief

Create your own visual expression: original copy, objects, composition and motion sequence. Use only your own or properly licensed assets. Do not carry over other brands, code, recognisable scenes or combinations of them. Say once, in the footer, that the exhibition is invented, and keep one note that the chair is not proof of durability or recyclability. Call the six items objects, not scenarios or concepts, and give headings a concrete part or object; use real facts only once the user provides reliable content.

The new project is MENDAVAL OBJECTS, an invented circular design exhibition. It tells how one everyday object can be taken apart, repaired and made again. Create six original exhibits. Do not make unverified environmental promises or pretend certificates. Each object's story must be clearly presented as a design scenario, not as facts about a product on the market.

Visual direction: matte yellow, rich ultramarine blue, a light chalky background and black text. Build bold typography: pair huge headings with small museum labels, play with scale and keep it readable. The main image is an original rebuildable chair made of wooden segments and contrasting joints. Start with the whole object, then show its materials, the logic of repair, a catalogue of six objects and a personal route the visitor builds. Avoid an agency portfolio structure and a game look.

Motion script:
1. When the first screen appears, the chair's segments come together into one object within 800 ms and stop.
2. Scrolling through the materials section, the seat, back and joints slowly separate, only within their own bounds; their labels appear beside them. At the end of the sequence the image stays calm.
3. When "Show the repair" is pressed, one joint of the chair is replaced with another within 500 ms and a short explanation appears; the action can be repeated.
4. When an object is added to the route, its number briefly travels to the route bar and the counter updates at once. With reduced motion, update only the state.

Build filters for the six objects by material and design theme. The visitor must be able to put together a route of 2–4 objects, change their order, remove choices and download the route text. If you use local saving, explain its limits in one clear sentence.

Work according to the existing development environment's instructions. The content is in English and original; no lorem ipsum, pretend partners, testimonials or awards. On a phone, replace the 3D scene with a compact image above the content. Respect keyboard focus and prefers-reduced-motion. Without WebGL, an SVG disassembly diagram and every catalogue function must work. Check the filters, the route order, the download, the 390 px and 1440 px layouts and the console. Deliver a working private preview. Do not publish publicly without a separate instruction.

This is a demonstration website with an invented working brand. All content and navigation must be reachable in ordinary HTML, independent of 3D. Use only your own, generated or clearly licensed images, models, sounds and fonts; in the final answer briefly state their origin. Do not stop at a plan: build the website and check a working private preview.
