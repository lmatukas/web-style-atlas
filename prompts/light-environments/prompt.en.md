## Original creative brief

Create your own visual expression: original copy, objects, composition and motion sequence. Use only your own or properly licensed assets. Do not carry over other brands, code, recognisable scenes or combinations of them. Say once, in the footer, that the brand is invented, and once beside any form or download that nothing is sent. Headings name the three spaces and the route rather than moods; use real facts only once the user provides reliable content.

The project is VELUMBRE, an invented working brand for a virtual exhibition of light installations. Build three distinctive artistic 3D environments: a sand amphitheatre, a fabric vault and a shadow valley. A shifting light direction must connect them, but each must have its own silhouette, spatial rhythm and way of exploring. This is a demonstration virtual exhibition: do not make up real artists, event dates, awards or ticket offers. Sound is optional.

Palette: dark petrol, mist grey and muted lavender. Use restrained large type and a lot of dark space. On the first screen, show a narrow vertical title strip, a wide view of the installation and a clear "Choose an environment" button. Then come three environment descriptions in ordinary HTML, a route of chosen stops and a short exhibition concept. The visible menu "Exhibition", "Environments", "My route", "About" must work independently of 3D.

Plan the motion concretely:
1. When the first scene appears, a band of light traces the amphitheatre's horizon within 900 ms and stops; the sequence runs once.
2. When an environment is chosen, the current scene darkens within 500 ms and a new stable composition replaces it. Do not make the visitor sit through a long camera flight.
3. Moving the pointer shifts the light and view direction of the chosen scene a little; when the pointer leaves, it returns to its starting state within 300 ms. On a phone, replace this with clear direction buttons.
4. Scrolling past an environment description, one distinctive element moves briefly: the sand contour, the fabric fold or the shadow. The motion stops at the end of the section; do not take over normal scrolling.

The working function is to choose environments, put them into a personal route, change the order of the stops and download a short route text. Save the choices in the browser and explain clearly that they stay on this device. Do not use fake sending or booking. Write the content originally in English.

Work according to the existing environment's instructions and make choices on your own. For phones, use compact scenes and large controls. Respect prefers-reduced-motion, the keyboard, visible focus and contrast. Without WebGL, provide original static SVG environments, all HTML content and every route function. Use only your own, generated or clearly licensed images, models and fonts; in the final answer briefly state their origin. Check the choices, the route order, the download, the 390 px and 1440 px views and the console. Do not stop at a plan: deliver a working private preview. Do not publish publicly without a separate instruction.
