## Original creative brief

Create your own visual expression: original copy, objects, composition and motion sequence. Use only your own or properly licensed assets. Do not carry over other brands, code, recognisable scenes or combinations of them. Mark the working demonstration name clearly as invented; use real facts only once the user provides reliable content.

Build a distinctive, working website for the invented working brand "Tembrelo", a workshop for handmade musical instruments. This is a demonstration concept with original English content.

Browsing logic: a clear vertical story in which one painted wooden ribbon travels through the whole website.

Palette: paper #F6F0E5, rich red #C54935, blue #233F62 and natural wood tones. Typography: a compact grotesque heading and a clear text face. At the start, a large fragmented macro view of an instrument on the left, with the heading and three instrument types on the right. Then "What sound is born from", three workshop stages, an interactive test of the instrument's character and an enquiry block. Avoid standard service cards and random decoration.

Build these specific animations:
• When "Try the ribbon" is pressed, the wooden ribbon briefly bends and returns to rest. A short sound plays only if sound has been clearly switched on.
• While scrolling through the making stages, the ribbon turns from straight into a light arc, then into the outline of an instrument body. At the end of each stage it stops so the text can be read.
• When one of the three instruments is chosen, the macro view changes with a horizontal wipe; the text changes in the same action, with no flicker.
• Clicking "Warm", "Clear" or "Rhythmic" changes a short sound sample, how tense the ribbon looks and a clear verbal explanation. No automatic background sound.

The main working action is to choose the instrument type, character and wood tone, then see a consistent enquiry summary. On a mobile screen, make the choices large buttons; reaching the content must not depend on dragging or sound.
