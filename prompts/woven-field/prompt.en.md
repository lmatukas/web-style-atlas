## Original creative brief

Create your own visual expression: original copy, objects, composition and motion sequence. Use only your own or properly licensed assets. Do not carry over other brands, code, recognisable scenes or combinations of them. Mark the working demonstration name clearly as invented; use real facts only once the user provides reliable content.

The project is LINTROVA, an invented textile design workshop. Its visual world rests on the tension of threads and the logic of weaving. The main image is an original field of thin threads from which the visitor can make their own abstract cloth pattern. It must be a clear graphic system with a limited palette, not a freely floating mass of colour. Create a working wordmark that could easily be swapped for another name.

Palette: natural linen, dark pine green and rust orange. Choose a restrained serif for headings and a clean sans serif for text. The layout should recall an open creative workbook: a narrow vertical table of contents, a wide main field and material fragments of different sizes. Sections: the idea, interactive pattern making, three original collections, the working process and a project enquiry draft. Do not build a standard grid of identical cards.

The animations must have a purpose:
1. When the page opens, the threads pull taut from two edges within 800 ms and stop with a barely visible leftover vibration.
2. Moving the pointer over the main field bends the nearest threads a little; when the pointer leaves, they return to a tidy cloth within 300 ms.
3. When the pattern density or a colour changes, the pattern re-weaves into the new state within 450 ms without reloading the whole page.
4. On reaching the collection section, its fragment briefly unrolls from one side; allow the effect only once for each section.

Build the pattern-making controls: density, two thread colours and one of three weave types. The result must really change and be downloadable as an SVG you created yourself. The pattern settings must appear in the enquiry summary. Without a server, offer to copy the summary instead of imitating a successful send.

Work according to the existing environment's instructions. The content must be original English, with no filler, pretend clients or awards. On a phone, control the interactivity with sliders and buttons and keep the text clear. With prefers-reduced-motion, show the state changes without distortion. Use SVG or Canvas; 3D is not required. Check the downloadable SVG, the keyboard, focus, the 390 px and 1440 px views and the console. Deliver a working private preview; do not publish publicly without a separate instruction.

This is a demonstration website with an invented working brand. All content and navigation must be reachable in ordinary HTML, independent of 3D. Use only your own, generated or clearly licensed images, models, sounds and fonts; in the final answer briefly state their origin. Do not stop at a plan: build the website and check a working private preview.
