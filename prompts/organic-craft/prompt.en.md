## Original creative brief

Create your own visual expression: original copy, illustrations, composition and details. Use only your own or properly licensed assets. Do not carry over other brands, code, recognisable scenes or combinations of them. Mark the working demonstration name clearly as invented; use real facts only once the user provides reliable content.

The project is KILNRUE, an invented ceramics studio with three illustrated forms: the Arc pitcher, the Low bowl and the Quiet vase. They are drawings, not tested products: give no capacity, watertightness, food-contact or care guarantees, no prices and no stock. Say so in a care note and footer. Warmth comes from irregular forms, generous space and specific language about surfaces, not from texture over the text or simulated handwriting.

Palette: warm paper #f6f0e3, a clay-sand surface #e7d7bf, dark brown text #35291e, muted text #645746, a terracotta accent #8d402c with cream text #fff4e3, borders #85745d and a rust focus outline #7a3e29. Use Georgia (or a licensed warm serif) for headings and Arial for body text and small uppercase labels.

Draw three original SVG objects with visibly uneven silhouettes, turning lines and fine speckle: a handled terracotta pitcher with an uneven rim and a pale glaze band; a low bowl with an olive-glazed interior and an exposed clay foot; a pale narrow-necked vase with a thin clay-coloured band. Place them on uneven pale backplates rather than rectangles.

Composition A, the making story: a compact studio wordmark with a tagline (clay / form / everyday) and visible links (the material, the making, our objects). A large left-aligned promise ("Objects with a little soul.") with one sentence and a link to the collection, beside an oversized pitcher on its backplate; a small angled annotation points to the material ("Not quite round. Just right.") and is not a button. A brief material statement with the bowl, a two-column making sequence of three numbered steps (find the form, leave a trace, make room for daily life), a quiet studio note with a link to the objects, and a native care disclosure.

Composition B, the shelf: a collection heading, one line and three local object links (01 / Arc, 02 / Low, 03 / Quiet). Unequal image stages: a large pitcher, a smaller bowl and a horizontal vase feature. Each piece keeps its name, a one-line use, a material note and a native "Study dimensions and material" disclosure (for example 24 cm high, 18 cm body width for the pitcher). A shared note on looking after real ceramics and a link back to the making story close the page.

Never replace the irregular stages with identical rounded product cards. On a phone each object stays whole above its text, the annotation moves next to its caption and the shelf becomes a single column that still varies object size.

## Final check

Check 360, 390, 768 and 1440 px widths for both compositions. There is no required motion: objects stay settled; prefers-reduced-motion removes short link transitions. Check keyboard focus, contrast on every real text background, the console and that there is no horizontal scrolling. A static screenshot alone does not prove that a control works; operate each one.

Functional acceptance: Material, process and object links reach real sections; each object's native disclosure shows its illustrative dimensions and material with no capacity or food-safety claim; no price, stock or purchase control.

Mark the demonstration project clearly in the page footer.

Deliver a working preview, a summary of the checks actually run, the origin of assets and the remaining limits. Publish publicly only when the user asks. Finish the implementation; a plan alone is not a result.
