Visual direction: a horizontal score of light membranes on a dark burgundy background with warm white and electric cyan accents. On the first screen, show the three readable sound layers at once: rhythm, texture and tone. Beside them, give a clear opening, "An eight-bar piece in three layers", and a button to turn sound on. Build four interactions. Only when "Turn sound on" is pressed do the membranes start pulsing to the chosen rhythm within 600 ms; when paused, the image settles calmly.
