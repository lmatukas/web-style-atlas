## Original creative brief

Create your own visual expression: original copy, illustrations, composition and details. Use only your own or properly licensed assets. Do not carry over other brands, code, recognisable scenes or combinations of them. Mark the working demonstration name clearly as invented; use real facts only once the user provides reliable content.

The project is ROOM FOR LOOKING, an invented independent art space showing "Exhibition 013", three original abstract studies. The works, sizes and venue are fictional; say so in the visit notes and footer. Do not borrow a real artist's identity, and if the user supplies real artworks, record artist, title, date, medium, dimensions and reproduction permission for each.

Palette: white walls #ffffff, a warm pale surface #f4f3ef, near-black text #202020, grey labels #595959, a charcoal accent #353535 with white text, grey rules #777777 and a blue focus outline #1643a0. Use one neutral sans serif (Arial or a licensed grotesque) at modest sizes; labels are small but readable, never microscopic or hover-only. The artworks bring their own colour; labels and architecture stay neutral.

Make three original SVG paintings with different proportions: "Interval in red" (tall — a red horizontal band crossing two dark verticals, with a small golden circle), "A folded distance" (low — blue triangular planes folding against warm grey along one sharp edge) and "A current, held" (wide — a pale curved ribbon moving across a terracotta field). No simulated frames or decorative shadows; never crop the works.

Composition A, the exhibition wall: a compact institutional header (the space's name, "independent art space · demonstration exhibition") with links (exhibition, work 01, visit notes). A modest exhibition title and one sentence. The three works sit at unequal wall positions, each with a label beside or below it (number / title, "fictional study, 2026", medium). Below the wall, a "Wall labels" chooser of three native radio buttons, styled with CSS so one work's note shows at a time; it must work without script. A narrow curatorial statement, a small SVG route plan (enter, pass works 01 to 03, step into the notes room; "not to scale"), a link to the work 01 record, and a native rights disclosure close the page.

Composition B, the single work: the selected painting fills a tall left field; a narrow right dossier carries "Selected work / 01 of 03", the title, a definition list (made, medium, format, method, expected life of the file versus a printed edition), one sentence of looking, and a numbered index of all three works (the current one marked). Below, a looking note explains one relationship inside the painting, a smaller companion work introduces the next piece with a link back to the wall, and a native collection-record disclosure closes the page.

Never turn the wall into rounded shop cards with price badges, and never put headings or buttons on top of the art. On a phone each work keeps its proportions at full width with its label directly below, the radio chooser stays above the notes, and the dossier follows the painting.

## Final check

Check 360, 390, 768 and 1440 px widths for both compositions. There is no required motion: content is present immediately; prefers-reduced-motion removes short transitions. Check keyboard focus, contrast on every real text background, the console and that there is no horizontal scrolling. A static screenshot alone does not prove that a control works; operate each one.

Functional acceptance: The three wall-label radios show one note at a time with keyboard and touch and without script; works keep their aspect ratio uncropped; the index and companion links reach real destinations; rights disclosures work; no purchase, booking or zoom action.

Mark the demonstration project clearly in the page footer.

Deliver a working preview, a summary of the checks actually run, the origin of assets and the remaining limits. Publish publicly only when the user asks. Finish the implementation; a plan alone is not a result.
