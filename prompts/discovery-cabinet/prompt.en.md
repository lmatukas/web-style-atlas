## Original creative brief

Create your own visual expression: original copy, objects, composition and motion sequence. Use only your own or properly licensed assets. Do not carry over other brands, code, recognisable scenes or combinations of them. Say once, in the footer, that the brand is invented, and once beside any form or download that nothing is sent. Headings name the seeds, jars and tray rather than "small thing, big discovery" slogans; use real facts only once the user provides reliable content.

The project is GRANOVEL, an invented exhibition about getting to know plants, made for families. The core experience is a round table seen from above with six distinctive seed jars. The visitor picks a jar, looks at a stylised seed and moves it to a virtual growing tray. This is an introductory artistic interpretation; do not make up biological facts, and present unverified growing conditions only as a clearly marked demonstration.

Use a light paper background, moss green, terracotta accents and dark ink. Build the objects procedurally or from original illustrations. Design the table geometry, textures and jar layout yourself. The first screen must explain the task in one sentence and offer "Open the cabinet" and "Show a simple list". Then present the exploring table, the tray of chosen seeds, short cards for looking closely and a downloadable discovery sheet.

Build four interactions:
1. When "Open the cabinet" is pressed, the jars spread out radially within 700 ms and stop; the content is not locked behind the animation.
2. When a jar is chosen, its lid lifts within 350 ms and the seed stays easy to look at; when it is closed, everything returns to its place.
3. When a seed is added to the tray, it settles into the chosen spot within 400 ms and the tray counter updates.
4. When "Show the growth sketch" is pressed, a stylised sprout appears in three short stages, then the animation stops. It is not an automatic, endlessly playing loop.

The working function is to build a personal discovery collection, remove items from it and download an originally formatted HTML or text sheet for looking closely. Dragging may be an extra method, but a clear button must also do every action. Save the collection only on the same device; say clearly if the data is kept in the browser.

Implement it according to the existing environment's instructions, write the original content in English and make decisions on your own. On a phone, provide large controls and a vertical collection. Respect the reduced-motion setting, the keyboard and visible focus. Without WebGL an equivalent SVG or HTML cabinet must work. Acceptance: all six seeds are reachable, adding and removing work, the download has content, and there is no horizontal scrolling or console error. Do not present invented partners or testimonials. Deliver a preview; do not publish publicly without a separate instruction.

This is a demonstration website with an invented working brand. All content and navigation must be reachable in ordinary HTML, independent of 3D. Use only your own, generated or clearly licensed images, models, sounds and fonts; in the final answer briefly state their origin. Do not stop at a plan: build the website and check a working private preview.
