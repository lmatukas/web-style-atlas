## Original creative brief

Create your own visual expression: original copy, objects, composition and motion sequence. Use only your own or properly licensed assets. Do not carry over other brands, code, recognisable scenes or combinations of them. Say once, in the footer, that the brand is invented, and once beside any form or download that nothing is sent. Headings name seasons, sizes and soil layers rather than slogans; use real facts only once the user provides reliable content.

Build a working, original, responsive website for the invented working brand "Glebora", a demonstration project for seasonal vegetable baskets and regenerative farming.

The new main motif is a relief of clay soil shown in cross-section, with three different layers and leaves growing on top. It is a warm, tactile object with no box or display-case shape.

Use fired clay #B65F43, cream #F2E6CF, dark olive #344634 and near-black #22251D. Large, clear heading type must sit well with comfortable reading of English text. On the first screen, a large heading "Baskets that follow the four seasons", a short explanation and the relief scene below. Then develop the story through a band of seasons, three farming principles, a choice of basket contents and an explanation of delivery. Write the content originally; present promises as a demonstration offer, not as confirmed facts about a business.

Build four considered interactions:
• The first time the visitor scrolls to the relief, the soil layers open sideways by 30–50 px and stop, revealing short notes.
• When a season is chosen, change the plant silhouettes and the basket produce within 500 ms; keep the scene camera fixed.
• When the pointer hovers over or focus lands on a layer marker, gently highlight only that surface and show one sentence. When focus leaves, return to the starting state.
• When the basket size changes, the produce rearranges into a new clear composition and the choice summary updates without reloading the page.

The visitor must be able to choose the season, the basket size and the delivery frequency, review a demonstration summary and submit an enquiry draft. No real payment or pretend order confirmation. If there is no server, explain the last step clearly.

Implement it in the user's project environment according to its instructions. Create your own procedural geometry and illustrations, or use licensed assets and record their origin. On a phone, drop complex 3D controls: control the content with large buttons. Ensure keyboard access, contrast, prefers-reduced-motion and a static soil section if WebGL is unavailable. Do not add fake farmer testimonials, certificates or client logos. Check 390, 768 and 1440 px widths, the consistency of the choices and the form errors. Deliver a working preview; do not publish publicly without a separate instruction.

Mark the demonstration project clearly in the footer. The main content and navigation must stay in ordinary HTML, reachable without canvas or 3D. Make sure you may use the chosen fonts, and in the final answer briefly state the origin of every asset. Finish the implementation; do not stop at a plan.
