## Original creative brief

Create your own visual expression: original copy, objects, composition and motion sequence. Use only your own or properly licensed assets. Do not carry over other brands, code, recognisable scenes or combinations of them. Say once, in the footer, that the studio is invented, and once beside the enquiry that nothing is sent; do not prefix every session with "demo". Headings name pieces, sessions and steps, not possibility or curiosity; use real facts only once the user provides reliable content.

The project is ORYNDEL STUDIO, an invented contemporary aerial dance collective. The core idea: movement builds a relationship with space. The visitor should get to know the creative direction, look through three concept performances and choose the format of an introductory session. Do not give the impression of a real organisation or of events that actually take place: mark the demo performances and schedule data clearly as concept content.

Visual direction: deep aubergine, warm peach and a light bone tone. Use original abstract illustrations of body movement, wide pauses and large editorial type. The first screen is an asymmetric fragment of a stage with one figure and the heading at the bottom left. Then: a creative manifesto, a vertical sequence of three performances, the session picker, the collective's method and a contact draft. No score tables or sports statistics.

Plan four motions:
1. On the first screen, a ribbon-shaped path draws the figure's route within 900 ms and stops; the heading appears in one continuous action.
2. While scrolling through the performance sequence, each illustration moves slowly within the space given to it; once the edge of the section is reached it stays still.
3. When a performance is chosen, its short description unfolds within 250 ms while the others stay clearly visible.
4. When the session format changes, the schedule rows rearrange within 200 ms; the user's focus is not moved arbitrarily.

Build working session filters by format and experience level. The choice must create an enquiry that can be copied with all chosen fields. If there is no booking system, show "Enquiry draft" clearly, not "Booking confirmed". Provide useful English content, with no invented awards, well-known clients or testimonials.

Implement it according to the existing environment's instructions and make decisions on your own. On a phone, present the content in order, with no forced screen rotation or scroll locking. Respect prefers-reduced-motion; animations must have static states. Every function must work without WebGL, with the keyboard and with touch. Check the filters, copying the enquiry, contrast, focus, the phone view and the console. Deliver a working private preview and briefly explain the original creative direction. Do not publish publicly without a separate instruction.

This is a demonstration website with an invented working brand. All content and navigation must be reachable in ordinary HTML, independent of 3D. Use only your own, generated or clearly licensed images, models, sounds and fonts; in the final answer briefly state their origin. Do not stop at a plan: build the website and check a working private preview.
