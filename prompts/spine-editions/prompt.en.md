## Original creative brief

Create your own visual expression: original copy, objects, composition and motion sequence. Use only your own or properly licensed assets. Do not carry over other brands, code, recognisable scenes or combinations of them. Say once, in the footer, that the brand is invented, and once beside any form or download that nothing is sent. Headings name the books, themes and the reading list rather than slogans; use real facts only once the user provides reliable content.

Build a finished English website for the invented working publishing project "RAŠTMĖ", which publishes small-run books about cities, architecture and the culture of everyday life. This is a concept demonstration with original sample editions. Work on your own according to the user's project environment instructions and deliver a private working preview. Do not publish publicly without a separate instruction.

Choose a palette of paper white, ink blue and vermilion red. The base is restrained editorial typography with solid support for Lithuanian characters. On the first screen, place one large fragment of a book spine vertically, with a short thought from the publisher beside it. Then build a catalogue of three themed shelves, a reading room for one edition's excerpt, a story of the publishing process and contact information clearly marked as a demonstration. Use margins and page-number motifs in the composition, not the logic of an agency portfolio.

Build four motions. When the site opens, the lines of the heading appear one after another within 600 ms and stay stable. When a catalogue theme is chosen, the editions rearrange within 300 ms, keeping the chosen state and keyboard focus. When a book is opened, the cover slides aside within 450 ms and reveals a readable excerpt; the same action must have a clear way to close. Scrolling through the process section, a margin mark moves to the stage currently in view without moving the text itself. Avoid constantly running text and hard-to-read letter distortions.

The main action is to find an edition by theme, open an excerpt and add it to a reading list. Save the list on this device and let the visitor clear it. Provide at least four original sample editions with consistent titles, short descriptions and content excerpts. Do not present invented author biographies as real, or use client logos, reviews or sales figures. Do not invent real sales.

On a phone, keep a comfortable line length for reading; content must not spill off the screen. Support semantic headings, the keyboard, visible focus, contrast and prefers-reduced-motion. A plain HTML catalogue and reading room must work without decorative effects. 3D is not required for this direction; do not use it just for effect.

The work is done when the filters, excerpts and reading list work, all text is original, and Lithuanian characters, the mobile view and reduced motion have been checked. Give the preview link and briefly name the checks and the design decisions you made on your own. All HTML content and navigation are reachable without 3D. Assets and fonts must be your own, generated or clearly licensed for use; in the final answer briefly state their origin. Without real sending, do not show "sent".

## Final check

Check 360, 390, 768 and 1440 px widths. In normal motion mode check the specific episodes and controls; check reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion`) and the required technology fallbacks separately. A static screenshot alone does not prove that an animation or function works. If a required technology is unavailable, state the limit exactly; do not present a decorative fallback as checked 3D or sound.

Functional acceptance: at least four original editions can be filtered, opened and added to a local list; clearing works.

Mark the demonstration project clearly in the page footer.

Deliver a working preview, a summary of the checks actually run, the origin of assets and the remaining limits. Publish publicly only when the user asks. Finish the implementation; a plan alone is not a result.
