## Original creative brief

Create your own house name, copy, monogram geometry and accessory drawings. Do not copy the logos, patterns or history of real luxury houses. Apply a real brand only with its approved details. One to three CC0 or public-domain images of museum objects may illustrate the craft; do not call them your products. Show the object's real approximate date and its illustrative purpose. Rights and source addresses belong in the rights file, not on the example page. Photographs up to 1.5 MB, WebP, without metadata.

Palette: paper cream #f4efe4, deep green #1f3a2f, ink text #26352c, warm brown #63402b. A local classic OFL serif 400 with a separate true italic, and a narrower sans 400 for text and uppercase labels. Up to two families and three files, up to 200 KB, WOFF2 with Lithuanian letters, full OFL.txt files. Labels 11–14 px with 2 px spacing; headings mostly 30–54 px, one campaign line up to 74 px. Plenty of paper space, thin lines, no decorative gold and no rounded-card template.

Signature: generate an original SVG monogram in real time from one to three typed letters. Use interlaced upright and italic letters, a round or diamond frame and woven-line geometry that depends on the letters. Typing ĄČŽ must produce real ĄČŽ in the mark, not a picture substitute. The timeline must work with natural scrolling and with native year links, by keyboard or touch. Do not lock scrolling.

Personalisation form: initials, thread colour Forest green / Warm brown / Dark wine, placement Centre / Lower corner. When any choice changes, the real object SVG, the monogram and the accessible image label update at once. Personalise only the original demonstration drawing of the folding case, never the museum photograph. Reject initials that are empty, numeric, symbols or longer than three supported letters. Latin letters and all Lithuanian letters are accepted; lowercase becomes uppercase. Confirmation requires a clear tick on the demonstration notice. An error moves focus to the right field. The summary names the exact object, initials, colour and placement, and says no order was created, no data was sent and no payment is taken. Changing a field hides the old summary. Restore returns ES, green and centre.

Care service: native disclosures for leather, silk and personalisation care. These are general notes for contemporary demonstration objects, not restoration of historic exhibits. Do not build a real basket, prices, order, payment or sending without a separate project instruction.

Motion: one 1.6 s appearance and settling of the monogram, with pause and a resume of the remaining episode. Reduced motion shows the finished mark at once; scrolling and choices stay fully usable. Stop the loop when the scene or window is hidden; on pagehide, free observers and listeners. No WebGL or audio is needed.
