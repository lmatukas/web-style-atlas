Build a working, original and responsive website for the invented working brand "Aurivane" — a workshop of sound artists who create spatial installations. This is a creative demonstration project.

The main new object is an instrument of seven matte copper acoustic plates that stands like a small sculpture. It has a clear form and directed side light; avoid random spinning abstraction.

Palette: warm ivory #F3EBDD, oxidised copper #9D5238, deep blue #142D40. Typography: a large humanist sans serif and a restrained serif for the story, using properly licensed fonts. The first screen is split into a narrow text column and a wide object scene; the menu is simple and visible at once. Original English content must explain what a visitor can commission. Then present three sound-space scenarios, a clear working process, an interactive choice of sound character and an enquiry.

Build the motion like this:
• When the scene loads, the plates line up from small offsets within 1.2 s and stop in their final composition.
• Moving the pointer shifts the light direction a little; the geometric scene stays stable. When the pointer leaves, the effect gently returns.
• Scrolling slowly into the scenarios, three plates separate and become markers of different spaces; after this transition every block has a stable image.
• Choosing "Calm", "Lively" or "Rich" changes the instrument's spacing and a short description. Sound starts only after a deliberate action by the visitor, with a clear way to turn it off.
