The [P10 outputs](docs/benchmarks/p10/README.md) retain authored consumer source;
the photo output materializes the existing atlas photographs by verified hash
instead of versioning duplicate image files. Its credits and rights remain with
the materialized project. The React output has its own retained package lock;
those dependencies are not part of the atlas runtime or a general stack recommendation.

For additions, record origin, source URL, creator, licence, local licence file,
redistribution permission, attribution and modifications before committing an
asset. Keep required notices beside fonts/images and update this summary when
third-party assets change. Unknown or unclear rights block inclusion.
