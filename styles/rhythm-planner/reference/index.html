    Your rhythm, your data

    How the timeline
    is worked out.

    Enter three tasks. The bars show how their lengths compare, and the gaps show the breaks you chose. There is no break after the last task.

    Midnight does not stop the plan: you will see a next-day mark by the time. Everything is worked out here, with no account and no server.

    Where does my plan stay?

    Only in the open page and in the file you download. Reloading the page brings back the example tasks.

    Will this make me more productive?

    It only adds up the times you enter. The real pace of your day is up to you.
