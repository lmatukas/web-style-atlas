THREE PERFORMANCE CONCEPTS

The form stays.
The relation shifts.

Original creative sketches. This is a demo vertical sequence, not a listing of real events.

01 / Distance / connection

Between two points

A concept sketch for a performance

Two imagined points of support and one path between them. This sketch explores how the feeling of space changes when the figure stops before the next step.

02 / Turn / choice

A break in direction

A concept sketch for a performance

A sudden turn of the line can change the whole composition. In this sketch a long arc meets a short pause, and the drawing keeps to its own space.

03 / Stillness / waiting

A pause in the air

A concept sketch for a performance

Not every movement has to end in a big gesture. In this concept the short gap matters most: the figure stays light, and the viewer's eye finishes its path.

THE COLLECTIVE'S METHOD

Notice.
Try. Stop.

  1. 01 / Watching

    Start with how a line changes an empty space.

  2. 02 / Trying

    Pick one idea and unfold it in a small gesture.

  3. 03 / Pause

    Leave time for the impression to settle. This page gives no instructions for physical exercise.

THE CREATIVE IDEA

Movement builds
a bond with space.

We start with a line and a question. How much room does a gesture need? When does a pause become part of the composition? Oryndel Studio is an invented collective that explores these questions here in original visual sketches.

Oryndel Studio draws movement for a collective that only exists on this page; its performances and times are concepts.

The figures are drawn as diagrams; this is not video. Code · MIT.
