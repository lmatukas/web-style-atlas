PERFORMANCES

Three pieces about distance,
turning and waiting.

These are pieces from the repertoire, not dated events.

01 / Distance / connection

Between two points

Repertoire piece

Two points of support and one path between them. The dancer stops before each step, and the room seems to change size.

02 / Turn / choice

A break in direction

Repertoire piece

A long arc ends in a sharp turn. The dancer holds still for a beat before the next arc begins.

03 / Stillness / waiting

A pause in the air

Repertoire piece

The figure hangs still between two movements, and the audience finishes the line in their heads.

HOW A SESSION RUNS

Each session has three parts:
watching, trying and a pause.

  1. 01 / Watching

    Start with how a line changes an empty space.

  2. 02 / Trying

    Pick one idea and unfold it in a small gesture.

  3. 03 / Pause

    Sit down and talk about what changed. This page gives no exercise instructions.

ABOUT THE STUDIO

Each piece starts
with one line in the air.

A piece starts as a line and a question: how much room does a gesture need, and when does a held moment become part of the dance?

Oryndel Studio, its performances and its times are invented for this design study.

The figures are drawn as diagrams; this is not video. Code · MIT.
