From question to object

We start with the space.

  1. We listen

    We define what happens there and what feeling you are after. One clear situation is enough.

  2. We test

    We compare the layout and the artistic sound character. A digital sketch is no substitute for an acoustic survey.

  3. We lay it out

    We prepare questions for a real conversation about material, place and the scale of the work.
