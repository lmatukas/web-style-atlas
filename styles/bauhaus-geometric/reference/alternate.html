FORM ASSEMBLY / EXHIBITION

Three studies
in form.

01 / Balance02 / Direction03 / Sequence
01

A point of balance.

A red disc meets a blue square. The empty paper around them does as much work as the shapes.

02

A change of direction.

A diagonal interrupts the square field. A smaller bar gives the eye a place to return.

03

One thing, then another.

Circles become a sequence through scale, spacing and one deliberate interruption. No animation is required.

THE PROCESS

Choose less.
Arrange with intent.

Three original compositions drawn in CSS for this demonstration. These are contemporary studies, not reproductions of historical works.
