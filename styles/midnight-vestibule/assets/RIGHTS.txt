The Commons records identify Highsmith and these reproduction numbers and mark the
photographs public domain. The Library of Congress rights statement
https://www.loc.gov/rr/print/res/482_high.html states that Highsmith dedicated her
photographs to the public domain. No endorsement or project authorship is implied.
The pages present the photographs as atmosphere for an invented hotel; they do not name
or describe the real buildings. The facade image is cropped so no lettering is visible.
Transfer: downscaled in a browser from the Commons 3840px derivatives and re-encoded
locally; the conversions create no new restrictions.
