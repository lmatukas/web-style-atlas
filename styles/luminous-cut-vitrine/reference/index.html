Craft

Fifty-seven planes.

Light enters through the table, bounces off the pavilion and returns through the crown. An angle off by a single degree cuts that journey short.

57facets in one stone

  1. 01

    Selection

    The stone is judged in daylight, before anything is measured.

  2. 02

    Cutting

    Crown and pavilion are matched to each other, never cut alone.

  3. 03

    Setting

    Six claws hold the stone and barely cover the girdle.

  4. 04

    Polishing

    The last step gives the metal a satin sheen, not a mirror.
