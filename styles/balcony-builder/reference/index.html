      COMMON QUESTIONS

      Before the first
      leaf.

      Can I buy a kit here?

      No. The picker and saving work, but there is no buying or ordering.

      How are the example plants chosen?

      The sunny option shows tomato sketches, plus pepper sketches if you have experience; partial light shows lettuce and spinach. With little light, this concept first offers a light-watching sheet and promises no plant set.

      The general light rule: university horticulture guidance. The low-light answer is a cautious rule based on university notes about shade. Exact varieties, container size and season need to be worked out for a real project.

      Where does a saved choice go?

      It stays only in the same browser on this device. In another browser, or after clearing its storage, it will be gone. The button removes only this record.
