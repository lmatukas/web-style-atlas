TWO FRAMES / ONE SHARED FEELING

A pause
between places.

A visual notebook about mist, water and the small space between one thought and the next.

01 / Into the mist02 / The last light

01

INTO THE MIST

The edges
become softer.

At first the trees seem to end where the mist begins. Look again and another layer appears. Nothing in the frame is in a hurry to explain itself.

Some places ask for a wider view. Others ask us to stay with what we can just barely see.

02

THE LAST LIGHT

For a moment,
everything holds.

The water carries a second sky. A narrow line of shore gives each half its measure. Then a small movement changes the whole picture.

This is a pairing of photographs from different locations, not a record of one continuous journey.
