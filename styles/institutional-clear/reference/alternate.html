Before you begin

A clear enquiry helps a service team understand what you need. Prepare:

  • A short description of the activity.
  • The expected group size and preferred date.
  • Any access or equipment needs to discuss.

Have access requirements? Include them in your enquiry so the space can be discussed before you make plans.

Room options

Match the activity to the room layout, then discuss capacity, access information and the facilities your group needs.

Meeting room
A setting for discussion and small group work.
Activity room
A setting for a class or community session.

How a request is reviewed

  1. Describe the activity

    Summarize the purpose, group and preferred time.

  2. Discuss the practical details

    Discuss suitability, access and any conditions before making arrangements.

  3. Receive a clear response

    A clear response should explain the decision, any conditions and the next steps.

Questions and help

Can I book through this website?

No. It is a design demonstration with fictional service information. There is no application or booking endpoint.
