Before you visit

Know what to bring to each area.

Short notes for areas B, C and D. Area A has its own guide.

BBorrowing event equipment

Name the event, the equipment needed and the expected return date. Think about collection, transport and who will take responsibility for the items.

CIntroducing yourself as a volunteer

Describe your interests, the kind of activity you enjoy and when you are generally available. A clear introduction helps the coordinator find a suitable conversation.

DSharing a community idea

Write a short description of the idea, who it would help and what support would be useful. Start with the outcome you want, then describe one practical first step.
