THE ATRIUM / AN IMAGINED ELEVATION

01 / THE IDEA OF THE HOUSE

Order at the edges.
Warmth at the centre.

A symmetrical arrival hall opens toward an imagined garden. The ornament follows its structure: repeated lines at the ceiling, a stepped silhouette above the entrance and an unhurried path to the rooms.

The house is a visual story, not a historical building record. Its character comes from the relationship between ceremonial public rooms and more private places to pause.
