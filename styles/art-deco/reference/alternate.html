THE ARDELUNE / FRONT ELEVATION

01 / THE IDEA OF THE HOUSE

A symmetrical hall
facing the garden.

A symmetrical arrival hall opens toward the garden. The ornament follows its structure: repeated lines at the ceiling, a stepped silhouette above the entrance and a long path to the rooms.

The house is a visual story, not a historical building record. Its character comes from the relationship between ceremonial public rooms and more private places to pause.
