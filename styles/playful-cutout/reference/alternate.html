THE SNIPWHISTLE STORY

A day that starts
with a scrap.

01 / Begin02 / Try03 / Keep going

01 / BEGIN WITH WHAT YOU HAVE

Start with any scrap
of paper.

There is no special starting point. A scrap of paper can become a small experiment, a question, or an invitation to look again.

Make one fold. Let it suggest the next.

02 / TRY ANOTHER WAY

Move the pieces
until one surprises you.

Move the pieces around. Keep the part you did not expect. Share an idea before it feels finished.

A making day works best when there is room for a wrong turn.

03 / TAKE IT HOME

Take one new idea
home with you.

You do not need a finished masterpiece. One new way of looking is a useful thing to bring home.
