01 / THE IDEA

BRING A HALF-FORMED
QUESTION.

Bring a half-formed question, an unfinished page or a different way to see the same old problem. You leave with a first version.

The programme moves between looking, trying and sharing. Its two days are separate routes through the same open question: what happens when an idea gets a little room?

01

LOOK.

Start with a close observation. Give an ordinary thing your full attention.

02

TRY.

Make a small version. Let the first attempt tell you what comes next.

03

SHARE.

Put the work into a conversation. Leave enough space for another voice.

GOOD TO KNOW.

There is no booking or registration.
