How much time should I keep for the evening?

Allow about two and a half hours. The menu times tell the story; the booking summary works out the end from your chosen arrival.

How do I mention allergies?

You can add a note with your booking choices. The demonstration only shows where it appears in the summary. On a real site, the restaurant team would confirm any menu changes separately.

Does this summary book a table?

No. This is a local demonstration for an invented room. There is no real sending, payment, stored data or booking system.
