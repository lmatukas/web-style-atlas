# Discovery Cabinet

## Intent

Discovery Cabinet turns close looking into a small collection-making task. The fictional Sėklų kabinetas is a family-oriented artistic exposition with six original seed-like forms. Visitors open a jar, inspect its shape, choose a tray position and carry a personal discovery sheet away as text. Names describe invented forms rather than botanical species. Questions invite observation without claiming germination rates, identification accuracy or growing instructions.

## Signature

Preserve five relationships: six distinct individual containers; an overhead radial table in A; readable lids that lift separately from their contents; a numbered selection tray; and an explicit, finite growth interpretation. The six illustrations differ through wings, a spiral, ridges, a drop, a star and a paired pod.  The drawer composition retains containers, practical controls and a personal tray while replacing the radial spatial arrangement with an indexed vertical selection route.

Collection data stays in this browser and survives reloads and composition changes.

## Adaptation

Replace the six illustrative objects with a coherent original or licensed collection while keeping individual containers, inspection and numbered selection. A craft kit, mineral study or museum handling set can use the same relationships with its own accurate descriptions. Verified educational facts need owner-provided sources rather than invented labels. Preserve a literal list alternative and an honest local-storage boundary in another platform. The full creation brief includes project, audience, content and composition fields, so a new project can retain the direction without copying the fictional seed names.

## Avoid

Exactly three deviations damage this direction: using six copies of one generic seed silhouette; making collection depend on dragging or hiding choices behind a decorative animation; and presenting the stylized growth sketch as factual biological prediction. Keep the collection export current and let visitors use the complete cabinet without WebGL. Local-storage failure must leave the in-page task and download available.
