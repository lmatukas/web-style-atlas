An instrument without ritual

Three voices.
Your interval.

Rhythm is short low beats. Texture is an original synthesised rustle. Tone is a soft sustained sound. Each has its own intensity.

One composition lasts eight bars and stops by itself. The tempo adjusts over one bar; volume and mute are always at hand.

What happens when you leave the instrument?

When the window is hidden or the instrument leaves the screen, sound and pulsing pause. When you are back, you choose when to carry on.

What if sound doesn't work?

Every choice, the recipe and the silent visual fallback keep working. Sound never starts by itself and nothing is sent to a server.
