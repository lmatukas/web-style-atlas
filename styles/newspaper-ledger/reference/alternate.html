ANALYSIS / PUBLIC SPACE

Twenty more hours
across four branches.

Four branch libraries and one proposal. What changes when opening hours are treated as a shared resource?

The Ledger data desk · Reading time: 3 minutes

across four branches

The question is when.

A library can be on the map and still be out of reach at the moment a reader needs it. Opening hours describe time as well as access. The proposal adds a half again to each branch’s current allocation.

That uniform increase makes the arithmetic simple. It does not tell us what a real library could staff, what readers would prefer or whether every neighbourhood would benefit equally.

More time is a proposition.
Better access needs evidence.

A real analysis would compare demand, staffing, transport and cost.
