SOURCE & METHOD

How are the measures calculated?

One bundled six-location array contains simulated Q1 and Q2 visit counts. Total is their sum; locations is the record count; largest is the maximum. Dots use a fixed 500-visit scale, so positions compare across rows and periods. Q1 is the comparison baseline. No network source or unique-person metric is implied.
