    Can you really walk this trail?

    These are three invented planning examples, not real routes anywhere. Places, distances and access are not checked. The diagram is not fit for navigation.

    How is the plan chosen?

    First, the plan fits the length you set and does not exceed the pace you chose. Among the remaining examples your mood comes first, then the longest day that fits. If the mood does not fit, this is explained clearly.

    Where are my choices kept?

    They stay in the open page and in the TXT you download. The list is not sent and not saved between visits.
