    § 01

    01 / Observe

    Write down a small thing.

    We collect everyday observations. First we look for a question we don't want to let go of.

    02 / Select

    Leave room for the reader.

    We shorten sentences and check how they connect. To us the margin is a place for your thoughts, not for missing text.

    03 / Compose

    Give thoughts a shape.

    We match the rhythm, the paper and the edge of the page. In this example the editions are original concepts, not goods for sale.

    A thought is worth a margin.

    What is real here?
