A lamp shaped

The Pebble is a low lamp with a chalk-white shell and a sage interior, drawn in one still view.

FORM / LIGHT / ATMOSPHERE

How the light falls
on the shell.

01

The window does the lighting

Light enters from the left wall. The upper edge of the shell takes the highlight, and the lower curve falls into shade.

02

The shadow sets it on the floor

A long, soft shadow runs to the right and fades before the far wall, so the object reads as resting rather than hanging.

03

Nothing moves to be seen

No rotation, loading sequence or moving camera. A viewer can compare the edge against the light in one still view.
