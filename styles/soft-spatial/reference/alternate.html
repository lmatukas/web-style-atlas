MORROW / THREE CONNECTED LAYERS

Space is a
feeling, too.

From a first impression to the final detail, we imagine environments in layers. Each one has a different job.

Walk through the layers ↓

01 / ATMOSPHERE

Set the tone.

Begin with the quality of a place: open or sheltered, warm or cool, social or still. A shared atmosphere gives the practical decisions a direction.

Focus
Light, color and mood
Study output
A spatial palette

02 / ARRANGEMENT

Make room.

Give each activity a place. Use a few deliberate volumes and enough space between them to make movement feel unhurried.

Focus
Scale, routes and relationships
Study output
A composition of volumes

03 / DETAIL

Bring it closer.

A rounded edge, a shallow recess, a surface you want to touch. Small decisions make the larger idea present at a human scale.

Focus
Edges, touch and transitions
Study output
A family of material details
