# Soft Spatial

## Intent

Soft Spatial introduces an object or a small practice through light, volume and breathing room. The fictional Morrow studio presents a Pebble ambient-object study, then explains three connected layers of spatial work. The page should feel approachable and tangible without claiming that a digital form is a manufactured product. Its dimensional quality comes from consistent illumination and well placed shadows, not from an expensive rendering engine or a continuous floating animation that competes with reading.

## Signature

Preserve four traits: a large softly rounded volume; diffuse directional highlights; a contact shadow that locates every object in space; and generous clear space between objects and practical copy. Chalk, muted sage and pale blush form a controlled material family. Text remains dark and flat rather than becoming another embossed object. The shallow opening in the Pebble gives its silhouette a recognizable interior. These relationships matter more than copying any particular pastel color or border radius.

## Navigation and interaction

## Adaptation

Choose a small number of coherent volumes and establish one lighting direction before writing decorative gradients. Keep the object’s scale large enough to explain the form, and label the work honestly as an illustration or a real product. A production practice can replace the fictional layers with verified services and actual enquiry destinations. The same HTML structure works in another stack without WebGL. Retain flat readable controls and modest practical labels while changing the brand or material palette.
