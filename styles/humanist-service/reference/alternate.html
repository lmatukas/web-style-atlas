A note about this example

Neighbour is a fictional digital-help service. The people, sessions and plan are demonstration content. No appointment is created and no personal information is sent.

What does this demonstration do?

The planning page changes its guidance to match your selected topic and session format. It shows a local summary, then lets you return to your choices. Reloading clears the plan.
