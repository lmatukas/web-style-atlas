THE OBSERVATION

There was nothing
to hurry towards.

The first useful thing was the pause. A narrow opening in the trees, a softer patch of light, a place to let the next thought arrive on its own.

We often give a journey its meaning afterwards. This imagined note keeps the smaller things: where attention moved, what changed when the pace slowed, and what stayed unfinished.

Not every useful direction
has to point somewhere new.
